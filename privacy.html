YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Your privacy and safety matter to us.

This policy is adapted from DSVSF’s published privacy policy, last updated 19 July 2026, for this static website. DSVSF should review and approve it before launch.

Information and this website

This site has no contact forms, accounts, analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process basic technical logs, such as IP addresses and requests. Check the host’s privacy policy for details.

Contacting DSVSF

When you call, email or message DSVSF, you choose what information to share. DSVSF’s published policy says limited case information may be recorded to provide support and accessed only by authorised people bound by confidentiality obligations. Avoid sharing sensitive details until you are comfortable with the channel and its privacy.

Donations and external services

Donation links take you to third-party payment providers, which process payment details under their own privacy policies. WhatsApp, email providers and other external sites also have their own practices. This website does not receive or store your payment details.

Use, sharing and retention

According to the published policy, information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide support, manage volunteering and comply with legal obligations. DSVSF states that it does not sell or rent personal information and may share limited information with consent, with service providers, or when required by law or to protect safety. Information is retained only as long as needed for these purposes or legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information and object to certain processing. Contact contact@dsvsf.org.ng, call +234 808 190 7305, or write to 5 Okanu Close, Okeafa, Isolo, Lagos State, Nigeria.

A note on safety

If someone monitors your device or internet activity, consider using a safer device or calling from a safe phone. This site cannot erase browsing history or guarantee private communications.

Read the published DSVSF privacy policy.
