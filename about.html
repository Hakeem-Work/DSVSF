ABOUT DSVSF

A safe place to be heard, believed, and helped.

We believe everyone deserves to live free from violence.

OUR PURPOSE

Standing alongside survivors.

The Domestic and Sexual Violence Survivors Foundation (DSVSF) is a Nigeria-based nonprofit working alongside survivors, families and communities to prevent abuse, respond with dignity and rebuild lives.

We provide counselling, legal aid, emergency shelter referrals and support in accessing justice. Our approach is grounded in compassion, confidentiality and respect for each person’s choices.
