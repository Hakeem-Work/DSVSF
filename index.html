WHO WE ARE

Everyone has a story.
We’re here to listen.

The Domestic and Sexual Violence Survivors Foundation (DSVSF) is a Nigeria-based nonprofit working alongside survivors, families, and communities to prevent abuse and rebuild lives.

With empathy at the heart of everything we do, we help survivors find safety, healing, and hope — free from judgment and free of charge.
